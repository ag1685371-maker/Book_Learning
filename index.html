Page 1

A Basket of Berries

One sunny morning, Bo the bear cub waddled into Clover Meadow. Beside the old oak tree he found a bush full of the reddest, juiciest strawberries he had ever seen. He picked and picked until his little basket was full to the top.

BoYummy, yummy! All of these berries are just for me!

Page 2

Two Hungry Friends

Pitter-patter, waddle-waddle. Along came Dot the duckling and Lulu the lamb. Dot's tummy gave a loud little grumble.

DotOh, my tummy is so rumbly. We looked everywhere, but we couldn't find any breakfast.

LuluThose berries look so sweet, Bo. You were lucky to find them.

Page 3

Bo Thinks Hard

Bo hugged his basket tight. He thought and thought. Then he looked at Dot's droopy little face, and he remembered how sad it feels to be hungry.

Bo thinksIf I share, I will have fewer berries… but my friends won't be hungry anymore.

Page 4

Enough for Everyone

Bo set his basket down on the soft green grass and smiled his biggest smile.

BoHere, friends! There are enough berries for all of us.

DotThank you, Bo! I'll bring cool water from the pond.

LuluAnd I'll bring my cozy blanket. Let's have a picnic!

Page 5

The Sweetest Picnic

So the three friends had a picnic under the big oak tree. They munched and giggled and told silly stories until the sky turned pink.

BoSharing made my berries taste even sweeter!

The lesson

Sharing makes hearts grow bigger

When we share, we may have a few less berries, but we gain more friends, more fun, and more love.
